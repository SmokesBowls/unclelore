## Arc Summary

The Cosmic Arc is the foundational cosmological layer of Chronicles. It encompasses the
destruction of Tiamat, the formation of Earth, the establishment of the Aeon Keeper oversight
system, the Nephoretti manifestation, the Graviton imprisonment, the Anunnaki occupation, and
the long arc of planetary recovery toward an unknown convergence event.

It is not a single storyline but a persistent background truth that every other arc operates
within.

## Arc Status

Active. The Cosmic Arc's events predate all character arcs but continue to exert pressure on
present-day storylines through:
- Graviton legacy (Vale)
- Aeon Keeper intervention history
- Vrill infrastructure damage from Anunnaki occupation
- Void Spire network (dormant/reactivating)
