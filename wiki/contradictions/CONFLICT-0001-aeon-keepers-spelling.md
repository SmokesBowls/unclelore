# CONFLICT-0001 — Aeon Keepers Spelling Variant

Status: resolved
Severity: low
Detected: 2026-05-07
Affected Pages: wiki/factions/Aeon_Keepers.md

---

## Conflict Summary

Two spelling variants of the Aeon Keepers' name appear across source files with no canon
decision resolving which is preferred or whether they refer to the same entity.

## Claim A

Source: book_01_book_of_genesis/001_the_ethereal_vigil.md (chapter body text)
Value: "Aeon Keepers"
Context: Used consistently throughout the chapter prose.

## Claim B

Source: Southern Arc metadata files (aaon keepers.txt)
Value: "Aaon Keepers"
Context: Appears in lore/reference files for the Southern Arc.

## Impact

Low narrative impact currently. If left unresolved across 27+ books it will create
inconsistent wiki entries and ingest noise. Should be resolved before large-scale ingest.

## Suggested Resolution Options

1. Confirm "Aeon Keepers" as canonical — treat "Aaon" as a typo.
2. Confirm "Aaon Keepers" as canonical — update all prose references.
3. Establish both as valid (different factions or eras) — requires further source evidence.

## User Decision

Canonical spelling confirmed: **Aeon Keepers**.

`Aaon Keepers` is treated as a typo / spelling drift alias only. It should not create a separate faction, species, era, or wiki entry.
