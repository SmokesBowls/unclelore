## Current Continuity State

Six named Aeon Keepers established as active, non-physical consciousnesses monitoring Earth's
recovery from the Tiamat shattering. They initiated the Nephoretti manifestation at the end of
the 3,417-year stabilization period.

## Canon Summary

The Aeon Keepers are non-physical entities who operate within the Ethereal Realm. They maintain
vrill circulation infrastructure and observe physical-world events through the Akashic Records.
They are capable of manifesting autonomous intermediaries — the Nephoretti — into the physical
plane as thought-seeds.

They do not appear to intervene directly in physical events. Their operational mode is oversight
and delegation.

## Identity and Nature

- Non-physical consciousnesses (do not incarnate into biological form)
- Operate within the Ethereal Realm
- Maintain geosynchronous anchor via the Akashic Records
- Responsible for vrill infrastructure continuity after Tiamat's destruction
- Can manifest Nephoretti as autonomous agents

## Historical Role

- Post-Tiamat: Maintained vrill circulation while Earth stabilized over 3,417 years.
- Observed Pelagor evolution into Proto-Giants.
- Decided to manifest one thousand Nephoretti as physical-plane intermediaries.

Later (from graviton_Canon.txt): Aeon Keepers intervened in the Graviton conflict — imprisoned
Gravitons within Void Spires to prevent catastrophic destruction. This places them in a
governance role beyond passive observation.

Also described in later sources as "beings of light and sound but wrong" by Graviton memory
(unresolved_lore.txt, Chapter 113-120 context). This may indicate a faction split or a
misidentification. → See UNRESOLVED: Aeon-Keepers-enemy-description.
