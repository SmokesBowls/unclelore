## What This Source Contains

Establishes the post-shattering Ethereal Realm and the operational role of the six Aeon Keepers
maintaining vrill infrastructure after the destruction of Tiamat.

Confirms:
- Earth forms naturally after the Marduk–Tiamat collision.
- The Aeon Keepers exist as non-physical consciousnesses within the Ethereal Realm.
- The Akashic Records remain geosynchronously anchored to the reforming planet.
- Vrill circulation requires non-physical operators.
- The surviving Pelagor evolved into massive geomantic proto-giants over 3,417 years.
- The Nephoretti are remanifested as autonomous intermediaries between ethereal and physical existence.

The chapter ends with the simultaneous manifestation of one thousand Nephoretti descending into
the physical world.
