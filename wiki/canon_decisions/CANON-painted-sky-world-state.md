## Canon Decision

The South Arc operates under an active painted-sky world-state after the relevant sky alteration event.

Natural sky descriptions such as "blue sky", "clear sky", "natural sky", or unqualified "sunlight" should be flagged for continuity review unless the chapter explicitly establishes an exception, false dawn, memory, dream-state, or pre-event chronology.
