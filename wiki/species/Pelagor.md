## Canon Summary
A species of octopus-like beings that once inhabited Tiamat's oceans and later evolved into massive, crystalline forms.

## Identity and Nature
Standing up to 40 feet tall, their bodies composed of living stone and minerals. They survived the shattering of Tiamat and have since evolved in underground caverns, retaining geomantic abilities but amplified. *(provisional — 2026-05-15)*
