# Nephoretti

Type: Species
Canon State: provisional

Last Updated: 2026-05-07

## Current Continuity State

One thousand Nephoretti manifested at the close of Chapter 1. They descend through the Veil
into physical existence as autonomous intermediaries between the Aeon Keepers and the
Proto-Giants. Their subsequent activity is established in Book 1 onward.

## Canon Summary

The Nephoretti are bioluminescent entities manifested by the Aeon Keepers as "thought-seeds."
They are smaller than the Giants and possess glowing skin. They arrived approximately 500 years
before the Anunnaki invasion.

From Nephoretti_north_Canon.txt (supplementary source):
- Primary roles: architects, mathematicians, teachers, and resistance strategists.
- Sustained by a "Prime connection" — a direct link to the Aeon Keepers in the Ethereal Realm.
- Possessed sophisticated vrill-manipulation techniques.
- Built pyramids (biological healing) and obelisks (harmonic singing).
- Devised the "Strategy of Friction" against the Anunnaki occupation.
- The Anunnaki's Needle spires throttled their Prime connection.

## Identity and Nature

- Bioluminescent skin (defining physical characteristic)
- Significantly smaller than Giants
- Manifested as thought-seeds — autonomous after manifestation
- Sustained by Prime connection to Aeon Keepers
- Can live for centuries
- Possess "future-knowledge" and spiritual guidance via Prime connection
- Sophisticated vrill-manipulation capability

## Members / Manifestations

- Total manifested at Chapter 1: exactly 1,000
- Named individuals established in later sources:
  - Senareth (resistance leader, Book 1 context)
  - Elyraen (resistance leader, Book 1 context)
  - Kyreth (first contact with Giants via water-spirals, Chapter 3)

## Historical Role

- Guided Giants from fear and isolation toward cooperation.
- Taught consciousness-touch to refine Giant elemental abilities.
- Architected the Garden Blooms era infrastructure.
- Shifted to military/logistical resistance after Anunnaki invasion.
- Gradually marginalized through the Anunnaki Sundering.
